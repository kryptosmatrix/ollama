## Why this loop stops here

Four review rounds of this instrument have found 14, 6, 8 and now 11 distinct blocking defects. The lenses are not drying (Method 20, rule 4), and round 4's findings sit in two places: behaviour the G1 blueprint does not specify (A1–A3: the terminal's refusal report, and whether a refusal must be reported for the failure number at all), and edges of mechanisms added in round 3 to answer round-3 findings (B1–B3, C1–C2, D1–D2). The first kind cannot be repaired in the instrument: any rule it adopts is a guess at an unstated requirement, and CONSTITUTION §16 routes a behaviourally significant ambiguity to the artefact that owns it, here the blueprint through OQ-4. The second kind is repairable, but a revision 5 made before the refusal requirements are stated would be re-reviewed against the same open question.

**Recommended order** (a technical sequencing decision under KANON 25; its outside check and reasoning are in the continuation-15 records): OQ-4 first, stating in the blueprint the terminal's refusal report for a missing capability, whether the no-dispatch trials require it, the desktop's refusal response, and the other open gaps (1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7); then revision 5 of the instrument against the stated text, with the repairs proposed above; its baseline against a fresh prediction; and round 5.

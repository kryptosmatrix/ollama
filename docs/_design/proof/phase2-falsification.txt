=== OUTCOME OF THE F9 INVESTIGATION ===
F9, F9b and F9c were three attempts to falsify Ollama-side process reaping. All three FAILED
TO FALSIFY: the protocol library's CommandTransport closes stdin, sends SIGTERM and then kills
on session close, and closes the transport itself when a handshake fails. There is no path in
which the manager's own per-server cancellation changed the outcome.
The cancellation machinery was therefore REMOVED, not kept. Unfalsifiable code that reads as a
safety measure is worse than no code, because the next reader believes the safety is ours.
What is kept: a manager lifetime context, justified by F8, which is the difference between a
server that survives its own connect timeout and one that dies the instant it finishes
connecting; and the two behavioural tests, because no-orphan is a promise Ollama makes to its
users and must keep holding if a transport is ever swapped or added.

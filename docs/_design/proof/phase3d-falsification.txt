=== TOKEN STORAGE ===
captured: 2026-08-06T04:31:50Z

FINDING, PROVEN BY EXPERIMENT — why there is no Keychain-backed store here.
The obvious way to reach the macOS keychain without cgo is the 'security' command. Its
add-generic-password takes the secret as a command-line argument (-w), with no stdin form.
A probe on this machine ran 'security add-generic-password -a probe2 -s ollama-argv-probe2
-w another-secret-98765' and grepped 'ps -ax -o command' while it ran: the secret was present
in the process list, readable by any local user. That is WORSE than a 0600 file, so the CLI
route is refused. A Keychain and DPAPI store needs cgo and is owed.
